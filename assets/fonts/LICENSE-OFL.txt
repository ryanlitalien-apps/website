The files JetBrainsMono-Regular-latin.woff2 and JetBrainsMono-Bold-latin.woff2
in this directory are a latin-subset, woff2-compressed build of JetBrains Mono
(upstream version 2.304), produced locally with fonttools (pyftsubset) for
self-hosting on this site. No glyphs were modified; only unused unicode
ranges were removed to reduce file size.
